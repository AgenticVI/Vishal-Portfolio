01 · Voice AI funnel

Improving the Voice AI lead conversion funnel

AI-led calls to insurance leads were converting badly, and nobody knew where the funnel leaked. I broke the Voice AI journey into measurable product outcomes, compared it side by side with the manual calling process, and improved the metric at each step.

  • Mapped both funnels step by step, from leads assigned to policies closed, and found the bottleneck: 44% of leads never picked up.
  • Research traced it to two causes: an un-rotated dialer number flagged as spam, and an internet-usage alert that made customers distrust the call.
  • After the fixes, not-picking fell from 44% to 30%.

Sep 2026 · PRD + MVP

Blinkit: getting users to try new categories

Most Blinkit users buy the same two or three categories, and 41% of surveyed users simply didn't know the rest existed. Search only matches product names, so a need like “study tonight” leads nowhere.

  • Wrote the PRD after a 50-person survey and six 1:1 interviews; defined the segment as “See & Add Shoppers” (about 13 Mn target users).
  • Proposed a non-monetary fix, AI-based Need Search with bundle suggestions (“Complete your muesli breakfast”), plus a need planner page with an Add all action.
  • Defined leading and guardrail metrics to track adoption and protect checkout conversion and orders. Target: new-category explorers from 22% to 30% a month.

Aug 2026 · Product teardown

ChatGPT Voice Mode: fixing feature blindness

Most people type to ChatGPT and forget Voice Mode exists. For Indian spoken-English learners prepping for GRE, TOEFL and IELTS, that's a free 24/7 speaking coach going unused.

  • Surveyed users (n=16): 81% forget Voice Mode exists, 77% prefer typing, and 73% feel speech anxiety talking to AI.
  • Mapped business outcomes to product outcomes and built an MVP of contextual nudges that offer Voice Mode right when practice makes sense.
  • Prioritised three ideas with RICE: contextual nudges won (7.5) over a voice-first entry point (3.9) and a named assistant (1.8).
  • Designed wireframes and an A/B test aiming at +15% voice adoption, +20% session frequency, +15% Voice CTR.

2026 · Product case

Myntra: from wishlist to purchase

Shoppers save items to wishlists and then never buy them. The question I asked was why, and what could nudge them across the line.

  • Built an AI-powered discovery engine that reads user reviews and conversations to find the purchase barriers: uncertainty, postponement and information gaps behind wishlist drop-offs.
  • Broke wishlist-to-purchase conversion into a KPI tree of leading and lagging indicators, turning behavioural signals into evidence-backed opportunity hypotheses.
  • Built an MVP, PeerPoll: pick wishlist items, turn them into a poll, and see the results, to track metric drivers and test interventions that reduce comparison friction and boost purchase confidence.

Jan 2026 · Machine learning

Patient churn prediction in healthcare

Clinics lose patients quietly. I wanted to see who was likely to leave, and why, early enough for a retention team to act.

  • Ran EDA to find the critical churn drivers: missed appointment rates, long travel distances and satisfaction score.
  • Built a supervised learning pipeline with Logistic Regression on standardised features and one-hot encoding to classify patients at risk of leaving.
  • Turned the output into actionable insights for targeted retention strategies.
